From the shop floor to the release note

Most product marketers learn CAM software from a brief. I learned it from the operator's chair — running pre-sales demos and post-sales training on SolidCAM and Mastercam, before that, programming CNC and VMC toolpaths on the shop floor for oil & gas and automotive components. That sequence is why the release stories I write for ModuleWorks hold up when an OEM engineer or CAM programmer actually reads them: the 5-axis, Swiss-Turn, and Turn-Mill claims are ones I've verified at the machine, not just in the brief.
